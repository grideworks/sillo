1. About Sillo

Sillo is a free game developed and published by Grideworks.

Contact: grideworks.games@gmail.com Developer location: Algeria

This Privacy Policy explains what information Sillo stores, how the game uses it, and what information may be handled by third-party services used in the game.

Sillo does not operate its own user-account system or backend server. Gameplay and profile information described below is stored locally on your device. However, third-party services integrated into the game—such as advertising providers and content delivery services—may collect information directly from your device when you use them.

2. Age Information

When you first open Sillo, the game asks you to enter your date of birth.

We use this information to determine your age group and adjust certain in-game features, such as content tone and notification options. The date of birth and/or age information is stored locally on your device and is not sent to Grideworks through a game server.

Age entry is self-reported. Sillo does not independently verify a player's age.

Age-related advertising and privacy settings are applied according to the game's configuration and applicable requirements. Entering a date of birth does not, by itself, constitute parental consent where parental consent is legally required.

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

Sillo may store the following information on your device to provide and maintain gameplay:

  • Date of birth or age information, used for age-based feature settings
  • Player name, if you choose to enter one
  • Selected avatar, from the game's built-in avatar images
  • Game progress, experience points (XP), and levels
  • Virtual currency balances
  • Records of virtual transactions within the game
  • Hint inventory
  • Settings such as language, theme, font, and sound
  • Notification preferences
  • Streaks and daily challenge progress

This information is stored locally using device storage (including SharedPreferences and app documents storage, where applicable).

Grideworks does not receive this locally stored gameplay information through its own server. The game does not provide cloud synchronization or a Grideworks-operated online account service.

4. Information We Do Not Directly Collect

Sillo does not itself request or maintain, through a Grideworks-operated server:

  • Your email address or phone number
  • Your real name
  • GPS location
  • Contacts
  • Photos from your device gallery
  • Camera or microphone recordings
  • Health information
  • Gameplay analytics or advertising profiles on Grideworks servers
  • Crash reports sent to a Grideworks-operated server

Some third-party services integrated into the app may independently process device or usage information, as explained in Section 5.

5. Third-Party Services

Sillo uses third-party services to provide certain features. These services may collect information directly from your device or browser when their SDKs, requests, or features are used. Their processing is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

### Google AdMob Purpose: Optional rewarded advertisements. AdMob may process information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, and ad interaction or diagnostic information, depending on your device, consent choices, and the SDK configuration.

Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

### Unity Ads Purpose: Rewarded advertisements, including as an alternative ad provider where enabled. Unity Ads may process advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, and information about ad delivery or interaction, depending on configuration and applicable settings.

Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

### jsDelivr Purpose: Downloading the map data file used by the game. When your device requests a file from jsDelivr, the service may receive technical request information such as your IP address, browser or device request details, and information necessary to deliver the file.

Privacy Policy: https://www.jsdelivr.com/privacy-policy

### Google Fonts Purpose: Loading fonts, if the game requests fonts from Google Fonts. A font request may disclose technical information such as your IP address to Google.

Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

### PayPal Purpose: Opening an external donation page, if you choose to use it. Any donation is handled by PayPal. Information you provide to PayPal is processed by PayPal, not collected by Sillo through its own server.

Privacy Policy: https://www.paypal.com/privacy

### External links Sillo may open external websites, email apps, or other services through your device. Once you leave the game, the privacy practices of the destination service apply.

6. Advertising

Sillo offers rewarded ads only. Ads are optional and are not intended to interrupt gameplay automatically.

Rewarded ads may be offered for in-game benefits, such as:

  • Receiving virtual currency
  • Choosing to continue, skip, or retry after a round, where available
  • Doubling a reward after completing a round, where available

The game does not intentionally display banner ads or interstitial ads.

Ad providers may process information to deliver ads, limit fraud, measure ad performance, and apply advertising choices. Whether an ad is personalized or non-personalized depends on the ad provider, the player's age-related settings, consent status, regional requirements, and the app's current configuration.

For children or users whose age requires child-directed or restricted ad treatment, the game must use the applicable provider settings and restrictions. Grideworks does not promise that an age entry alone can prevent all inaccurate age declarations.

Sillo does not offer a paid in-game option to remove advertisements.

7. Notifications

Sillo may schedule local notifications on your device. These notifications are generated by the app on your device; they are not push notifications sent from a Grideworks server.

Notifications may relate to:

  • Daily challenges
  • Streak reminders
  • Available in-game rewards
  • New shapes or game content
  • Weekly tasks
  • Educational facts
  • Reminders after a period of inactivity

You can manage notification categories within the game's settings and through your device's notification settings. Notification availability and permission requirements depend on your Android version.

8. Children and Family Privacy

Sillo is designed to be suitable for a broad audience. We do not operate a Grideworks server to collect children's personal information.

Because the game includes third-party advertising services, their SDKs may process information as described in their own privacy policies. Advertising SDKs must be configured to apply the age-appropriate treatment, consent requirements, and restrictions relevant to the player's age and location.

Where parental consent or other protections are legally required, an age entry alone is not a substitute for those requirements. Parents or guardians can also use the device's available privacy and advertising controls.

9. Sharing Your Results

If you choose to use the in-game Share feature:

  • The game creates a shareable image locally on your device.
  • Android opens its system sharing interface.
  • You choose whether to share the image and which app to use.

Sillo does not upload the image to a Grideworks server. The app you choose to share through may process the image and information according to its own policies.

10. Map Download

Sillo may download a static map data file from jsDelivr.

  • The map file is downloaded for use by the map feature.
  • The download request may disclose technical information, including your IP address, to the CDN.
  • After the file has been downloaded and saved successfully, the map feature is intended to use the local file without repeatedly downloading it, unless the file is removed, needs to be updated, or the app's implementation requires another request.

The map data and map-related attribution are provided in accordance with the applicable source licenses and attribution requirements.

11. Data Storage, Retention, and Deletion

Gameplay and settings information described in Section 3 is stored locally on your device and retained until you delete it using the in-game data deletion option, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the game (subject to Android/device behavior and any backups managed by your device).

Sillo does not provide Grideworks-operated cloud backups or account-based synchronization.

You can use the in-game Delete All My Data option, if available, to clear locally stored game data. Uninstalling the game also generally removes app-local data. Third-party services may retain information they independently collect according to their own policies.

12. Your Choices and Controls

You can:

  • Edit your in-game profile, where available
  • Change game settings and notification preferences
  • Disable notifications through the game or Android settings
  • Use device or platform controls to manage advertising and privacy choices, where available
  • Delete local game data using the in-game deletion option, if available
  • Choose whether to use optional rewarded ads
  • Choose whether to share your results or open external links

13. Security

Sillo stores gameplay data locally and does not maintain a Grideworks-operated database of player profiles.

Local app storage is not a guarantee against access by someone who has access to your device, or against risks from a compromised device. Please use appropriate device security, such as a screen lock.

Third-party services are responsible for the security and processing of information they handle under their own systems and policies.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the game, its features, or its services change.

The updated policy will be published with a revised “Last updated” date and version number. Where appropriate, important changes may also be communicated in the game.
